# Vítor Cimonetti

PhD student in Physical Education at the State University of Londrina (UEL), Brazil, working in biomechanics and human movement analysis.

My research focuses on markerless motion capture, ground reaction force prediction and musculoskeletal modeling in people with knee osteoarthritis.

## Background

- MSc in Physical Education, with a focus on biomechanics (UEM/UEL).
- Postgraduate training in Data Science (Albert Einstein) and Neuroscience (UEL).
- Member of the Applied Biomechanics Laboratory at UEL.
- Experience with gait assessment, OptiTrack/Motive, Visual3D, force platforms and OpenSim.
